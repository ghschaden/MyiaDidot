# Approved Roman old-style figure drawings

These GLIFs preserve the Regular drawings and proportional advances
approved in `proofs/roman-oldstyle-redesign-2026-10-25.pdf` on 2026-10-25.
They derive from Myia Didot's Theano-based Roman figures (see OFL.txt),
with revised height bands, width and optical stroke weight. The original
angular 3, open 4 and curled ball-terminal 9 are retained.

They are fixed design inputs for `scripts/oldstyle_figures.py`:
`space_figures.py` retains this reviewed fit when rebuilding the Regular
figure family, and Bold inherits it through the normal generator.
