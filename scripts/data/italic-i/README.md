# Approved lighter Italic I

`I.glif` is the −15 thick-stroke trial from
`proofs/italic-ri-weight-review-2026-10-25.pdf`, approved
after the assistant recommended retaining the old drawing. That numerical
recommendation is superseded by the visual judgment.

The fixed outline is in final spacing. `scripts/italic_i.py` installs it
in final spacing, preserving the existing small caps and Greek mark fit.
Advances, anchors and metadata remain those of the generated glyph.
Bold Italic inherits the accepted base through its normal weighting.
The r trial was not approved and is not installed.
