# Regular acute-counter review, 2026-10-25

The [comparison proof](../proofs/regular-acute-counters-review-2026-10-25.pdf)
shows all four styles and enlarged Regular joins. Eight exception entries
represent four constructions: M, lowercase Cyrillic м, trademark and numero.
Greek Mu and Cyrillic М are identity components of M; Ḿ and Ṃ add marks to M.

Each flag is the open white tip between a thin upright and an adjoining
diagonal, below the top join. These channels are open to the surrounding
counter, not detached ink, enclosed pinholes or failed attachments.

The corresponding regions match the actual Theano reference outlines
exactly: zero XOR area in a 170 × 150 unit crop around each finding.
M and м use a −20-unit x translation reflecting Myia spacing; trademark
and numero need no translation. Whole M and numero also match exactly.
The whole м and trademark outlines have small differences outside the
reviewed region, so this review does not claim whole-glyph identity there.
