On the way the designer gave two rules, now in `docs/claude/quality.md`:
a straight stem is one thickness from top to bottom and never steps in
where it meets its serif; a bowl's inside is a clean curve. The first
state of the Bold Italic's small-cap D at the x-height and of ы.sc's
bowl was refused for them.

Accepted anew with this review: every accepted glyph of the Italic (116)
and the Bold Italic (113) that had changed. Not every one of them is on
a sheet: those that moved by less than the sheets' thresholds are in
`build/redraw/sweep-*.txt` of that day (not kept in git), by units.

Left open: whether the bowls' outsides that still run into Old
Standard's diagonal are made round; ы.sc's bowl overlapping its second
stem; the Bold Italic g's advance, 7 units wider, and the hint zones of
G and Ɠ at 35 ppem, both untraced.
