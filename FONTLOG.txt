FONTLOG for Myia Didot
======================

This file provides detailed information on the Myia Didot font software.
This information should be distributed along with the Myia Didot fonts and
any derivative works.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

Myia Didot is a derivative of Theano Didot by Alexey Kryukov, part of the
Theano Classical Fonts (https://github.com/akryukov/theano). Theano Didot
is a Didot-style face for Greek (with the full polytonic set), Latin and a
basic set of Cyrillic.

Myia Didot aims to extend it to complete coverage of Latin-script
alphabets, Greek and basic Cyrillic, and to add Italic, Bold and Bold Italic
styles as well as true small capitals.

Because the Theano fonts carry "Theano" as a Reserved Font Name, this
derivative is published under a different name, as the SIL Open Font
License requires.


ChangeLog
---------

2026-10-14 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 1: review fixes (turned r, Ɑ, Ϗ, ʑ, breve)

    * ɹ (and ɺ ɻ built on it): the arm is no longer r's ear turned upside
      down (a hanging hairline) but a Didot terminal, a hairline leaving the
      stem low, turning down to the left, thick when upright, into a ball
      on the baseline (attach takes "curl" pieces); ɻ's hook continues the
      stem.
    * Ɑ: the bowl of O set a little inside the cap height, so its hairlines
      run cleanly into the stem. Ϗ: K's leg and its foot serif cut higher,
      the tail continuing the leg's direction and weight.
    * ʑ: the bar turns up into a loop and comes down through it into an
      upright tail below the baseline, as in Charis.
    * The Latin breve (Theano's, as wide as the circumflex) is a fifth
      narrower and a little shallower at the same weight (new
      scripts/adjust_accents.py); ă ğ Ắ ... follow, and the tallest
      stacks are lower (win ascent 2278 -> 2235).

2026-10-14 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 1: sample documents

    * U+2010 HYPHEN and U+2011 NON-BREAKING HYPHEN added (the hyphen's
      shape): babel hyphenates with U+2010 in several languages, and the
      hyphens dropped out.
    * New proofs: proofs/samples.tex (book pages, the UDHR's first article
      in 17 languages, Vietnamese, African orthographies and IPA, Greek,
      features, sizes; in `make proof`) and `make udhr`, one quarto page per
      language for 43 languages (scripts/udhr_book.py).

2026-10-14 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 1: Vietnamese tone marks beside the circumflex

    * As habitual in Vietnamese typesetting (Trương, Vietnamese
      Typography): the acute, grave and hook above go to the right of the
      circumflex, from halfway up its right arm, instead of on top of it;
      the tilde stays above, breve combinations stay stacked. New combined
      marks circumflexcmb_acutecomb/_gravecomb/_hookcomb (and .cap, on the
      spacing circumflex_acute ... Circumflex_Hook), used by composites.py
      for Ấ Ầ Ẩ Ế Ề Ể Ố Ồ Ổ and their lowercase and small caps, and formed
      from decomposed text by the new ccmp lookup ccmpVietnameseToneMarks,
      so both render alike. These letters are about a fifth lower.

2026-10-14 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 1: checked against Libertinus

    * Every character was compared with Libertinus Serif (Math for the
      rest) on vertical extent, width, counters and ink. Fixed: Ƹ dips
      below the baseline like Ʒ; the clicks ǀ ǁ ǂ stand on the baseline
      (they were | and descended), ǁ's bars closer; the retroflex hooks
      (ɖ ɭ ɳ ɽ ɻ ʈ Ʈ Ɽ) stop at -420 instead of the full descender, as in
      Charis and Libertinus; ˞ redrawn as an ear rising to the right, so
      ɚ ɝ carry it visibly and joined; ʐ's tail deeper; Ɣ's loop below
      the baseline; ꜛ ꜜ rebuilt from the mended arrows (no gap); the
      seagull ̼ joined; ˈ ˌ ̍ ̩ at stem weight; ƴ's hook higher; 𝑙 spaced
      on its ink. Differences inherited from Theano Didot (У and Ф with
      tails, the iota subscript under capitals, its accent sizes) are kept.

2026-10-14 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 1: the last small caps

    * Small caps for Ƙƙ Ɲɲ Ϗϗ Ʝʝ (from the capitals, as the others) and the
      saltillo Ꞌꞌ (the straight quote at small-cap height). ǭ.sc is built
      in smallcaps.py's specials from ǫ.sc and the capital macron on its
      TopCap anchor, as ō.sc: Ǭ ǭ are upstream specials without anchors,
      so the accent builder could not make it. Two small-cap NFD
      exceptions (U+01EC, U+01ED) are gone with it.

2026-10-14 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 1: the last letters and letterlike symbols

    * Ƙ (the hook on the arm, as on Ƴ), Ϗ (the leg run on below the
      baseline and turned back), ʝ Ʝ (the tail crossed by a hairline; Ʝ
      on the tail of ȷ), ℓ 𝑙 ℮ ℵ ℶ (scripts/draft_letterlike.py). Drafts.
    * Review fixes: ɸ is no longer the open Greek φ but o with a stem
      through it (as in Charis); ɰ is u joined to ɥ (the descender on the
      stem); ɹ (and ɺ ɻ) keep only the right half of the foot serif, which

    * 𝔐 𝔓 𝔖 𝔭 are Libertinus Math's Fraktur (SIL OFL 1.1, The Libertinus
      Project Authors), adapted: scaled to Myia's cap height and x-height,
      the vertical strokes brought to Myia's stems, the hairlines made a
      little finer, and the hairline ends given Myia's ball in place of the
      pen's tip (draft_letterlike.py; chosen by the person over drafts
      after Unger's classicist Fraktur).
      Credited in OFL.txt and the copyright notice. With these the Phase 1
      coverage target of Regular is complete.

2026-10-14 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 1: small caps at 1040, ẞ

    * Small caps raised from 960 to 1040 units (73% of cap height, 156 above
      the x-height), redrafted with 176-unit stems; the height is stored in
      the UFO lib (org.myiadidot.smallcapHeight) and every tool reads it.
      Comparison of 960/1000/1040: scripts/smallcap_heights.sh.
    * Added ẞ (U+1E9E), drafted from ß: scaled to cap height, vertical stems
      thickened to the capital weight with the hairlines kept; marked as a
      draft for review.
    * ß and ẞ take a small ẞ in small caps; ss02 ("Small caps: SS for ß and
      ẞ") gives the traditional SS.
    * Cedillas joined to their letters (Ç Ş Ţ Ȩ and lowercase, small caps):
      upstream left a gap of 56-68 units; the tip now runs 30 units into the
      bottom stroke (combining cedilla anchors lowered by 110/122; the
      spacing ¸ is unchanged). Comma accents stay detached, as is usual.
    * Small-cap ŀ: dot placed as in Ŀ (it had drifted past the foot); typed
      l·l in small caps is kerned to look like ŀl.
    * Small-cap ľ: apostrophe placed like in Ľ by the same rule (it ran past
      the advance width into the next letter).
    * Capital dot accent (Ż İ Ė Ġ Ċ and the dotted small caps) enlarged
      from 192 to 224 units, scaled about its bottom so the gap is kept; the
      lowercase dot (248) made it look undersized. Chosen after comparing
      192/224/248. Diaeresis unchanged.

2026-10-14 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 1

2026-10-14 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 0

    * Imported TheanoDidot-Regular.sfd from akryukov/theano at commit
      ff01bf7506ca672ca121792fc472ed527503a907 (2022-01-31, Theano 2.0);
      the pinned source is kept in sources/upstream/.
    * Renamed the family to Myia Didot.
    * Converted the source to UFO 3 (cubic "Fore" layer) and moved the build
      to fontmake. The hand-made quadratic "TTF" layer of the SFD is not
      used; TrueType outlines are now converted from the cubic outlines and
      hinted with ttfautohint.
    * Renamed the 106 legacy afii* glyph names to uniXXXX names
      (sources/glyph-renames.json).
    * Class kerning, GDEF classes and ligature carets now live in the UFO
      (kerning.plist, groups.plist, public.openTypeCategories, caret
      anchors); kern, mark, mkmk and GDEF are generated at build time. The
      Greek "accent before capital" positioning, which upstream kept in a
      FontForge-specific required feature, is now part of kern.
    * Verified against a FontForge build of the unchanged upstream source:
      852,414 test strings shape identically except 11 degenerate sequences
      with U+0344, where the diaeresis and the acute are now stacked instead of
      overlapping.
    * The Graphite (.gdl) source of upstream is not carried over.
    * Vertical metrics unified: OS/2 typo, hhea and win ascender/descender
      are all 1962/-630 with zero line gap (USE_TYPO_METRICS set). Upstream
      had typo 1580/-468 + 560 gap but hhea 1962/-630 + 560 gap, which
      spaced lines differently on Windows and macOS. The new line height
      (2592 units) is within 1% of upstream's Windows line height.
