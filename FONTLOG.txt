FONTLOG for Myia Didot
======================

This file provides detailed information on the Myia Didot font software.
This information should be distributed along with the Myia Didot fonts and
any derivative works.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

Myia Didot is a derivative of Theano Didot by Alexey Kryukov, part of the
Theano Classical Fonts (https://github.com/akryukov/theano). Theano Didot
is a Didot-style face for Greek (with the full polytonic set), Latin and a
basic set of Cyrillic.

Myia Didot aims to extend it to complete coverage of Latin-script
alphabets, Greek and basic Cyrillic, and to add Italic, Bold and Bold Italic
styles as well as true small capitals.

Because the Theano fonts carry "Theano" as a Reserved Font Name, this
derivative is published under a different name, as the SIL Open Font
License requires.


ChangeLog
---------

2026-10-27 Development: quality check for wobbles against the source

    * scripts/quality/checks.py `wobble` (the Bold, the Bold Italic): a
      curve bending back the other way for a short stretch (3 degrees or
      more within 160 units, between bends the first way) more often than
      in the glyph it is generated from. It finds the Bold ψ φ of before
      today's fix (and not after), Bold Italic г ʷ, and o's inner side;
      absolute curvature measures flagged hundreds of drawn Didot
      transitions (serif brackets, italic S-strokes) instead.
    * Bold 169 and Bold Italic 375 glyphs have one more than their source:
      recorded as candidates (FONT-DESIGN.md); most come from the handle
      fit in emboldening, to be fixed in the generator.

2026-10-27 Development: quality check for steps on the outline

    * scripts/quality/checks.py `step`: a straight edge of at most 70
      units jogging aside (turning 40 degrees or more from both) between
      two edges that run on the same way (within 35 degrees): a tail
      narrower than its stem, a hook's end cut standing out of the
      letter. It finds every step fixed today in the Bold Italic (ʈ ɳ ɚ
      ɝ on the outlines before) and ʋ's, still open; glyph_lint's raster
      steps missed them.
    * The current findings are listed as candidates to review (Regular
      11, Italic 16, Bold 14, Bold Italic 23 glyph entries; FONT-DESIGN.md).
    * It found Bold Italic ſt's crossbar arm cut to a 36-unit stub: the
      cut making ſt of ﬅ reached past the wider f stem into t's arm
      (italic_ligatures.py). It now stops where t's arm begins: arms as
      t's (Italic 131, Bold Italic 91).
