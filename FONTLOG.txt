FONTLOG for Myia Didot
======================

This file provides detailed information on the Myia Didot font software.
This information should be distributed along with the Myia Didot fonts and
any derivative works.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

Myia Didot is a derivative of Theano Didot by Alexey Kryukov, part of the
Theano Classical Fonts (https://github.com/akryukov/theano). Theano Didot
is a Didot-style face for Greek (with the full polytonic set), Latin and a
basic set of Cyrillic.

Myia Didot aims to extend it to complete coverage of Latin-script
alphabets, Greek and basic Cyrillic, and to add Italic, Bold and Bold Italic
styles as well as true small capitals.

Because the Theano fonts carry "Theano" as a Reserved Font Name, this
derivative is published under a different name, as the SIL Open Font
License requires.


ChangeLog
---------

2026-10-20 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 3: Bold spacing against the references

    * Every frequent Latin, Greek and Cyrillic pair of every style now lies
      within the references' range (scripts/quality/pair_summary.py),
      but four of the Bold Italic (of Wh it ba): measured tight, they look
      right beside Bodoni Moda, and looser they open into gaps (reviewed,
      left as they are).
    * Bold: tr ag xt kt, loose, kerned tighter. The Greek stood tight in
      most pairs of σ τ η (right side) and κ (left side) against the Greek
      references, as the Bold's Latin sets them. They now have sidebearings
      of their own (σ +40, τ +35, η +45, κ +50, their accented forms with
      them), and εχ ελ are kerned. Bold Italic: ft tighter, δη ρη ρυ ημ
      looser.

2026-10-20 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 4: brackets, rounding, joins

    * Where emboldening overran a concave bracket into a straight edge (the
      Bold Italic G's jaw, some 400 serifs that had curled into hooks, the
      Bold e's bar), the bracket's node slides along the edge until the
      bracket is as long as before, and its handles grow with it. The
      bracket's other end now runs on along the stroke it leaves: the
      source's slight corner there (about 3 degrees, on handles too short
      to show it) no longer shows as a kink. Bold Italic kinks 655 -> 597.
    * Rounding to whole units no longer breaks smooth joins in the Bold and
      Bold Italic: where it turns one by more than a degree, a handle moves
      a unit to keep it in line (make_bold.py round_smoothly). Smooth nodes
      broken by more than 2 degrees: Bold 177 -> 66, Bold Italic
      757 -> 306. The Italic, which the Bold Italic is made of, is rounded
      as before.
    * Ҳ: in the italics the tail met the foot serif in a step; the serif's
      top now runs straight onto the tail's corner. The Bold Italic's ⱥ.sc
      is a.sc and its slash joined (a.sc's own bracket at the foot), no
      longer emboldened whole, which thickened the joins.
    * The Italic's kept glyphs (reviewed or drawn) were respaced on every
      run of make_italic.py (г.loclSRB lost 80 units each time); they are
      now kept as they are.

2026-10-20 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 4: guillemets, horns, в, dieresis

    * The guillemets ‹ › « » drawn as Didot chevrons, angled and heavier
      than the thin ones before (scripts/draft_symbols.py --guillemets);
      the italics take the Regular's, sloped.
    * The Vietnamese horn heavier (0.67 of the comma, the capital's 0.79),
      and 1.2 times as large again in the italics, near the reference
      italics' weight. Ư and its accented forms grow the horn out of the
      stem, the serif's outer arm cut away, in every style.
    * Bulgarian в: the upper bowl's floor level from the stem, without the
      dip β's waist left; its counters are cut clear of the pieces
      attached into them.
    * The dieresis on the capitals (Ä Ö Ü ...) has larger dots (by 192/174),
      no longer smaller than the lowercase's.
    * All drafts reviewed and accepted, in every style.

2026-10-19 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 4: a Didot bold's contrast

    * Bold and Bold Italic emboldened by stroke weight: the thick strokes
      gain the same in every direction, the hairlines stay hairlines.
      Before, every upright stroke gained a stem's weight (N's hairlines
      64 -> 157 units) while thick diagonals ending in a crotch gained
      little (N's +37): N M И W and their small caps looked light, the
      hairlines heavy. Now N's diagonal 275 -> 381, its hairlines 67. The
      letters made small (superiors, numerators, modifier letters, ª º)
      are emboldened at their scale; accents as before. Each glyph's side
      space is fitted to the white the earlier Bold gave it, so text sets
      as before (line lengths unchanged); Greek σα σω σφ and Cyrillic ње уд
      ал kerned where they fell outside the references.
    * G's jaw: the bar on the spur shortened (60 % inside the counter,
      25 % outside), the advance by what it gave up; Ɠ Ǥ ₲ ǥ ɠ rebuilt.
      ₲ and ₵: the bar centred on the bowl and on the C.
    * The Vietnamese horn brought up to an accent's weight (0.56 -> 0.72
      of the comma's, near Old Standard's); the 29 modifier letters rebuilt
      by their recipes (ᶿ's bar 17 -> 36 units). All drafts in the Regular's
      review sheet accepted.

2026-10-19 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 4: serifs apart in the Bold Italic

    * Emboldening brought neighbouring serifs of the Bold Italic together
      only partly: v.sc's, w.sc's and y.sc's top serifs, ʌ.sc's feet,
      Π's, Ш's, h.sc's and a.sc's met in a notch over a joint a few units
      thick (ʌ.sc's feet over 4 units). Such serifs are now kept apart,
      their tips shortened before emboldening until at least half of the
      Italic's gap stays open (scripts/make_bold.py part_serifs; 40 letters
      and their accented forms, and ™ in the Bold). Serifs joined over
      their whole height (x.sc's inner feet) stay joined. Ľ before W now
      sits 8 units closer, the room the shorter serifs give.
    * The CFF hinting has a test of its own: letters on a zone must fall on
      their reference letter's row exactly (tests/test_hinting.py,
      hint-zones-cff*.txt; only Œ in the uprights, and ẞ ƹ ъ 𝔖 in the
      italics, stay up to 0.3 px off).

2026-10-19 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 4: hinting

    * TrueType: ttfautohint now runs with a control file made for each
      style (scripts/hint_control.py). The thick diagonal of M N Μ Ν М м
      and their small caps ends under the left serif in a point
      ttfautohint did not see; when the serif was rounded to a whole pixel
      the point stayed behind and poked up to 0.84 px above the cap
      height (9 to 16 px). It now moves with the serif. A's apex (and Ą Ʌ)
      snapped to the ascender zone, a pixel above O, at 15, 16 and 20 to
      34 px; it is moved onto O's row. The Bulgarian forms with ascenders
      (ж ъ ь ю) are hinted with the Latin zones (ttfautohint's Cyrillic
      has no ascender zone), and so is the Greek of the italics, whose
      x-height zone was raised by π σ τ: ε ο ρ stood a row above x. Signs
      outside any script (brackets, bars, daggers) are hinted as Latin
      rather than only scaled. Letters ending off their zone between 8 and
      32 px, in FreeType's two TrueType interpreters: Regular 90 and 60
      before, 24 and 14 now; Bold 12 and 17, now none; Italic 468 and 512,
      now 41 and 52; Bold Italic 434 and 484, now 13 and 39 (tests/
      test_hinting.py holds them, scripts/quality/hint_zones.py).
    * CFF: the OpenType fonts had no stem hints, and every style carried
      the Regular's zones and stems (the Bold's StdVW was 52). Zones and
      stems are now measured from each style's glyphs and the fonts hinted
      with otfautohint (scripts/ps_hints.py): with FreeType's CFF engine
      the tops and bottoms of the reference letters now all fall on whole
      pixels, on one row per zone.

2026-10-19 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 3: ʼ and ʻ spaced as letters

    * The modifier letters ʼ (U+02BC) and ʻ (U+02BB), the apostrophe of
      Navajo, Hawaiian and many African orthographies, stood so near a
      following i that ʼi read as a double quote; in the italics they hung
      past their advance and touched an l. Regular and Bold: ʼ 50 units
      more on the left and 90 on the right, ʻ 50 and 60, as far from l
      and i as ’ and ‘. Italics: both moved about 140 units left
      (measured upright). ˮ keeps its two marks as near as before; Ľ ľ ŉ
      ģ and the combining comma marks are unchanged. Found by the new
      language corpus (UDHR in Navajo).

2026-10-19 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 3: Cyrillic spacing

      Charis and Libertinus agree against Myia (the roman т о с, the
      italic л м), Old Standard and EB Garamond side with Myia; the new

      (Vietnamese without hook/horn, letters with dot/macron/circumflex/tilde
      below, double grave, inverted breve, caron series, Ӣ ӣ Ӯ ӯ, ...), using
      scripts/composites.py, which follows upstream's accent conventions and
      reproduces upstream's own composites.
    * Anchors completed: Acute/Grave/Dotaccent wherever only Top existed,
      Cedilla anchors recovered from upstream's cedilla composites, and the
      Top anchors of C, G, g, y, К, у moved by 2-20 units to match the hand-
      tuned upstream composites (decomposed text now matches precomposed).
    * Stacked top marks: TopMark/_TopMark anchors recomputed from each mark's
      ink, with a 90 unit gap (60 on capital accents), so a Latin acute sits
      clearly above a diaeresis or tilde. Polytonic Greek is not affected.
    * Fixed an import error: FontForge's feature export had turned the
      Cyrillic breve context (И/У + breve) into a rule that deleted the base
      letter; it only showed where text is not recomposed (й + stress mark).
    * Win ascent/descent now cover the bounding box (2280/630); the new
      stacked capitals were clipped on Windows before.
    * Added Ȯ and Ẉ (case partners of the new ȯ and ẉ); Ĭ İ Ī follow the
      anchor (4 units).
    * Regression test suite (tests/), pre-commit hook and CI; Claude Code
      plugin with status line, guards and commands (tools/claude-plugin).

2026-10-14 Version 0.100 (development), Phase 0

    * Imported TheanoDidot-Regular.sfd from akryukov/theano at commit
      ff01bf7506ca672ca121792fc472ed527503a907 (2022-01-31, Theano 2.0);
      the pinned source is kept in sources/upstream/.
    * Renamed the family to Myia Didot.
    * Converted the source to UFO 3 (cubic "Fore" layer) and moved the build
      to fontmake. The hand-made quadratic "TTF" layer of the SFD is not
      used; TrueType outlines are now converted from the cubic outlines and
      hinted with ttfautohint.
    * Renamed the 106 legacy afii* glyph names to uniXXXX names
      (sources/glyph-renames.json).
    * Class kerning, GDEF classes and ligature carets now live in the UFO
      (kerning.plist, groups.plist, public.openTypeCategories, caret
      anchors); kern, mark, mkmk and GDEF are generated at build time. The
      Greek "accent before capital" positioning, which upstream kept in a
      FontForge-specific required feature, is now part of kern.
    * Verified against a FontForge build of the unchanged upstream source:
      852,414 test strings shape identically except 11 degenerate sequences
      with U+0344, where the diaeresis and the acute are now stacked instead of
      overlapping.
    * The Graphite (.gdl) source of upstream is not carried over.
    * Vertical metrics unified: OS/2 typo, hhea and win ascender/descender
      are all 1962/-630 with zero line gap (USE_TYPO_METRICS set). Upstream
      had typo 1580/-468 + 560 gap but hhea 1962/-630 + 560 gap, which
      spaced lines differently on Windows and macOS. The new line height
      (2592 units) is within 1% of upstream's Windows line height.
